### Option 1: Neutrino mass limits and decaying dark matter
**Category:** astro-ph.CO  
**ArXiv ID:** 2603.03284v1  
**Authors:** Thomas Montandon, Vivian Poulin, Thomas Rink  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03284v1

**Abstract:**
We revisit cosmological neutrino mass bounds when a fraction of dark matter is allowed to decay to massless dark radiation. By compensating the late-time increase in the matter density induced by neutrino mass, decaying dark matter can partially relax the tension between CMB and low-redshift probes.

**Quick Take:** Explores intersection of neutrino physics and dark matter decay. Relevant to precision cosmology tensions.

### Option 2: Beyond thresholds: reconstructing UV physics from IR expansions
**Category:** hep-th  
**ArXiv ID:** 2603.03277v1  
**Authors:** Hiromasa Takaura, Wen Yin  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03277v1

**Abstract:**
We show that ultraviolet information can be extracted from low-energy expansion coefficients, assuming analyticity and the absence of massless singularities. By reorganizing the low-energy expansion using dispersion relations and unitarity bounds, we demonstrate systematic recovery of UV physics from IR data.

**Quick Take:** Theoretical method for extracting fundamental physics from low-energy observables. Foundational impact.
