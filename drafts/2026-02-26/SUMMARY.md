## 📋 Paper Options

### Option 1: MOND Cosmology on Large Scales (astro-ph.CO)
**Title:** How does a MOND cosmology fare on Gpc scales? - Collisionless N-body simulations of νHDM  
**Authors:** Alfie Russell, Indranil Banik, Oscar Cray, Hongsheng Zhao  
**arXiv:** [2602.21975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21975)

**Why it's interesting:** Largest N-body simulations testing MOND gravity theory at cosmological scales. Rules out νHDM variant at >5σ confidence, directly comparing alternative gravity to ΛCDM.

**Key insight:** MOND models overproduce structure and create conflict with observed bulk flows—fundamental challenge to modified gravity theories.

### Option 2: BMN-like Matrix Models (hep-th)
**Title:** BMN-like Matrix Models  
**Author:** Eunwoo Lee  
**arXiv:** [2602.22163](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22163)

**Why it's interesting:** Extends the canonical BMN matrix model (bridge between string theory and gauge theory) to more general AdS₄×X₇ backgrounds with N=1 supersymmetry.

**Key insight:** Advances holographic duality beyond standard AdS/CFT, enabling study of quantum gravity in diverse pp-wave geometries.

---

### Option 3: Quantum Vacuum Choice in Inflation (gr-qc)
**Title:** Choice of Quantum Vacuum for Inflation Observables  
**Authors:** Melo Wood-Saanaoui, Rudnei O. Ramos, Arjun Berera  
**arXiv:** [2602.22116](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22116)  
**Published in:** Symmetry 18, 399 (2026)

**Why it's interesting:** Tests whether alternative quantum vacuum states (α-vacua) beyond Bunch–Davies could modify inflation observables. Compares theory with Planck satellite precision data.

**Key insight:** α-vacua are stringently constrained by observations—Planck data limits departures from standard vacuum choice, constraining fundamental quantum gravity effects.
