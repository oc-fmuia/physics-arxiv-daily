# Paper Option 3: Observational Cosmology

**arXiv ID:** 2603.02177v1  
**Title:** Interpreting map-based E/B spectral properties of CMB foregrounds  
**Authors:** Gilles Weymann-Despres, Léo Vacher, Michael E. Jones, Angela C. Taylor, Carlo Baccigalupi, A. J. Banday, Richard D. P. Grumitt, Nicoletta Krachmalnicoff  
**Category:** astro-ph.CO  
**Submission Date:** 2026-03-02  

## Abstract

Map-space E/B decompositions of linear polarization are attractive for foreground and CMB analyses because they isolate the B-family patterns that contaminate primordial tensor searches from E-family patterns that trace coherent Galactic structures. However, the E/B transform is non-fully-local and induces apparent spectral complexity in projected fields even when the underlying sky is spectrally simple in P=Q+iU. We quantify this effect for synchrotron emission. We introduce a complex-parameter description of the frequency dependence of P, its spin-preserving projections P_E and P_B, and the scalar S=E+iB, using complex log-Taylor and moment expansions (with simple transformation rules under E/B projection) and linking their coefficients to spectral-index variations, line-of-sight mixing, synchrotron ageing, and Faraday effects. This work is intended to be of practical relevance for both Galactic science and CMB B-mode analyses and lays the conceptual foundation for a series of papers applying the framework to observational data.

## Key Insights

- Critical methodology for separating primordial CMB signals from Galactic foregrounds
- Develops new tools for B-mode analysis essential for detecting gravitational waves
- Provides framework for optimizing future CMB experiments
- Addresses systematic uncertainties in next-generation surveys

## Relevance

Crucial for unlocking the potential of next-generation CMB observations. B-modes from primordial gravitational waves are a key target for cosmic inflation research; this work removes major systematic barriers to detection.
