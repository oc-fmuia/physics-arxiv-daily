# Selection Guide: Which Paper to Feature?

## Quick Decision Matrix

### For Maximum Audience Impact: **Option 3** (Quantum Vacuum)
- Already published in a peer-reviewed journal (Symmetry)
- Directly uses Planck satellite data (observational confirmation)
- Bridges quantum mechanics, gravity, and cosmology
- Suitable for both specialists and informed general audience

### For Cutting-Edge Science: **Option 1** (MOND Cosmology)
- Largest simulations of its kind
- Directly challenges alternative gravity theories with decisive evidence
- High-impact negative result (ruling out νHDM)
- Strong appeal to cosmologists and dark matter researchers

### For Theory Enthusiasts: **Option 2** (BMN Matrix Models)
- Pure theoretical physics advance
- Extends a foundational model in string theory
- Smaller immediate audience
- Strong appeal to quantum gravity researchers

---

## Recommendation Criteria

**Choose Option 1 if:**
- Focus on observational/empirical results
- Audience includes cosmologists and general physics enthusiasts
- Want to cover "what doesn't work" (negative results are valuable!)
- Interested in dark matter debates

**Choose Option 3 if:**
- Focus on precision physics and data comparison
- Want a published, peer-reviewed story (stronger credentials)
- Bridge quantum foundations with observations
- Audience values both theory and experiment

**Choose Option 2 if:**
- Audience is primarily high-energy theory community
- Interest in foundational structures of gauge/gravity duality
- Want to feature emerging theoretical directions
- Less concerned with immediate observational relevance
