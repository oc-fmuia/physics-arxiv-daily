## Abstract
We conjecture a family of matrix quantum mechanical models that are holographically dual to discrete light-cone quantization of M-theory in pp-wave-like backgrounds. These backgrounds can be obtained from a Penrose limit of AdS₄×X₇, where X₇ is Einstein. The matrix models arise from a classically consistent dimensional reduction of the UV Lagrangians of N=1 superconformal field theories, in close analogy with how the BMN matrix model is obtained by dimensional reduction from N=4 super Yang-Mills theory. We also discuss about supersymmetric black objects in pp-wave background by studying the Witten index and speculate that the area of the horizon is bounded from above for a fixed N.

## Why This Paper?
This paper extends the well-known BMN matrix model—a fundamental tool in holographic duality and string theory—to more general settings. It provides new insights into the relationship between matrix quantum mechanics and M-theory, connecting AdS/CFT-like dualities to pp-wave geometries.

## Key Contributions
- Generalization of BMN matrix models to AdS₄×X₇ backgrounds
- Connection between dimensional reduction and holographic duality
- Analysis of supersymmetric black objects in pp-wave backgrounds
- Constraints on horizon area in terms of N

## Significance
This work advances our understanding of the gauge/gravity duality beyond the standard AdS/CFT framework, with potential applications to studying quantum gravity in pp-wave backgrounds.
